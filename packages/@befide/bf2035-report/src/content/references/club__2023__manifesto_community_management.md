  abstract: >-
    Research and Knowledge Organisations are impactful primarily through their
    capacity to bring people together. Therefore, Community is crucial to the
    generative function and success of Research and Knowledge Organisations. And
    so, Community formation and maintenance, as undertaken by Community
    Managers, is a consequential form of labour within Research and Knowledge
    Organisations.
