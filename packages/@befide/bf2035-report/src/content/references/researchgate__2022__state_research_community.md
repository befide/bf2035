  abstract: >-
    Earlier this year, we asked the research community to share their
    experiences around work/life/science balance and career prospects, academic
    freedom and participation, and inclusivity and community support in our
    first State of the Research Community survey, co-developed with the German
    Center For Higher Education Research And Science Studies. With your help, we
    were able to gather over 10,000 responses. We are pleased to announce that
    the data is now accessible to organizations and scholars that are interested
    in conducting their own research. Please note: we removed any open ended
    responses from the data to prevent any potentially personally identifiable
    information from being exposed.
